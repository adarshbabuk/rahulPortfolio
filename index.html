About

Rahul is a Human Capital Consultant with around three years of industry experience. His work spans Total Rewards, Organizational Development, Performance Management and HR Analytics.

He is currently an Associate in People Strategy & Governance at TIAA | Nuveen Bank, where he works on operating-model design, benefits benchmarking, labour-law compliance and capability building.

Prior to TIAA he was with Deloitte, working closely with industry leaders on Human Capital solutions and co-leading sector coverage for the Human Capital Consulting team. At Altimetrik he partnered with the CHRO and the leadership team to advise on HR strategic initiatives — from competency frameworks to hybrid work models.
